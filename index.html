Trip Descriptions

#Trip Description
1A perfect mix of Southern California city life, mountain charm, and beach relaxation. Start in San Diego, wind through the Ojai Valley, and end with sunsets on the Santa Monica coast.
2For those who love the ocean. Follow the Pacific coastline through beach towns—San Diego, Laguna, and Santa Monica—for a relaxing, sun-soaked drive with ocean views the entire way.
3A simple, low-stress plan: base in San Diego for two nights, then head north to Santa Monica as your coastal home base. Great for biking, day trips to Malibu, and easy Christmas dining.

Visual Keyword Guide by Location

  • San Diego: Gaslamp Quarter, Old Town, La Jolla Cove, Sunset Cliffs, Balboa Park, Christmas lights.
  • Laguna Beach: Heisler Park, Crystal Cove, art galleries, surf coves, tide pools, scenic cliffs.
  • Ojai: Spanish arcade, Meditation Mount, vineyards, orange groves, Los Padres mountains, Pratt Trail.
  • Santa Monica / Malibu: Pier, Third Street Promenade, Ocean Avenue, Solstice Canyon, Malibu Creek, beaches, palm walks.
